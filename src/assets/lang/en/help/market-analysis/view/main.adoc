The *market analysis* tool shows how a data product behaved over a selected period of time.

== Workflow

. Pick a data product from the list
. Choose a time period for data that will be used for calculations
. Select the period for the ATR and the timeframe
. Inspect the table

//image::market-analysis.png[Market analysis overview]

== Reading the table

The columns have the following meaning:

|===
|Field|Description

|Date|Day of the calculation
|Close|Closing price at the end of the session
|Close change (%)|Closing price respect to the price of the previous day (that is, [p(t)-p(t-1)]/p(t-1))
|True range|True range of this day
|SQN(100)|System Quality Number calculated on the last 100 days
|ATR|Average of the true range, over the selected number of days
|ATR(%)|Average of the true range divided by the price of the day
|Direction|Classification of the market's direction
|Volatility|Classification of the market's volatility
|===

The direction is classified using the SQN into:

- Strong bear
- Bear
- Neutral
- Bull
- Strong bull

The volatility is classified using the ATR into:

- Quiet
- Normal
- Volatile
- Very volatile

NOTE: 100 days are required to calculate the SQN(100) so
, the time period must include these days. A value of 6 months (180 days) will output just 80 rows or less.
