= Market Analysis

The *market analysis* tool shows how instruments behaved over a selected period.

== Workflow

. Pick a data product from the list
. Choose a timeframe and a period
. Inspect the generated charts

image::market-analysis.png[Market analysis overview]

== Reading the chart

* The upper panel shows the *price* series
* The lower panel highlights *volume* and *volatility*

TIP: Use the zoom controls to focus on a specific date range.
